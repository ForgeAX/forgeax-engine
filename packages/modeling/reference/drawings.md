# Drawings

Part of the [`@forgeax/engine/modeling` reference](../README.md).

## Drawings: views, design drawings and contours

Three-view blockouts start from 2D outlines drawn in the orthographic views. The six
views share `ORTHO_VIEWS` frames (Y up, the subject's front faces +Z); a view point is
`u = p . right`, `v = p . up`, depth `w = p . toward`. `model.render` orients its
cameras with the same frames, so contours and design drawings line up with the renders.

| View | `right` | `up` | `toward` (to the viewer) |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| `front` / `back` | `+x` / `-x` | `+y` | `+z` / `-z` |
| `right` / `left` | `-z` / `+z` | `+y` | `+x` / `-x` |
| `top` / `bottom` | `+x` | `-z` / `+z` | `+y` / `-y` |

| Surface | Contract |
|:--|:--|
| `Drawing` | The design drawing as data (no images), the single owner of 2D design intent: `{ name, sources?, views, items }`. A view is `{ id, ortho }` (an `ORTHO_VIEWS` frame) or `{ id, section: { axis, at } }` (a station; across z it reads like `front`, across x like `right`, across y like `top`). An item has an `id`, its `view`, a closed `role` (`DrawingRole`: `package`, `silhouette`, `feature`, `section`, `break`, `pivot`, `linkage`, `structure`, `guide`, `construction`), optional `link` (items in other views with the same link are one 3D element), `basis` (`public`, `blueprint`, `estimate`, `derived`), `tolerance` and `note`, and is one of: `point` (`at`, optional `depth` along the view's toward axis), `curve` (`curve: CurveSpec`, `closed?`, `continuity?`), `dimension` (`measure`: `distance`/`dx`/`dy`/`angle`/`radius`, `refs` of 0 to 2 items, or for an angle 3 points `[a, vertex, b]` in its own view (the unsigned angle at the vertex); `value` and `tolerance` in meters, radians for angles), `note` (`refs`, `text`). `CurveSpec`, open or closed: `polyline` keeps the points; `spline` samples a centripetal Catmull-Rom through them until the chord error is within the tolerance; `fit` reduces dense samples (a traced outline) to the fewest points within the tolerance of every sample (`smooth: true` passes a spline through the kept points); `arc` (`center`, `radius`, `from`/`to` radians) and planar `nurbs` (a kernel `NurbsCurve`, z = 0). Unknown fields fail |
| `resolveCurveSpec(spec, { closed?, tolerance? })` | The one curve sampler (`resolveDrawing` samples every curve item through it): a `ResolvedCurve` `{ kind, closed, points, deviation, tolerance, bounds }` within `tolerance` (default 2 mm); `deviation` is the fit's distance to its samples (0 for the other kinds). A closed curve drops repeated and closing points, needs a non-zero area (`model-degenerate`) and is oriented counter-clockwise |
| `m.extrudeContour(d, id, { depth: [near, far], grow? })` | Prism of the closed curve item `id` of drawing `d` (in an ortho view, sampled within the item's tolerance) along its view axis between two view depths; roles `side`, `top` (toward the viewer), `bottom`; sharp rims. The session keeps the resolved `Contour` (`ResolvedCurve` plus item `id` and `view`) for drawings |
| `m.threeView(d, ids, { margin?, clearance?, label?, material? })` | Three-view intersection: two or three closed curve items of `d` on distinct view axes; each is extruded through the others' extent (`margin` overshoot, tag `view-<view>`) and intersected with the result so far, so history and replay show every prism and cut. Views share extremes by construction, so contour i grows outward by i x `clearance` (default 0.5 mm) and the result follows every contour within 2 x `clearance`. Dense smooth fits make sliver-heavy prisms; prefer straight `fit` outlines within a few millimeters for the TS boolean baseline |
| `resolveDrawing(d)` | Validates ids (unique across views and items), views, refs, links (at most one item per view), roles and geometry, and samples every curve in its view within its tolerance (default 2 mm; a bounded total of sampled points). Dimensions get their drawn span from their refs: two points, one point (`dx`/`dy` from the view's axis) or an arc's radius. `drawingViewFrame(view)` gives the view's frame and plane depth, so `fromView(frame, p, depth)` / `toView(frame, q)` map view coordinates to world points (`fromView`/`toView` take an ortho view name or a frame) |
| `m.drawing(d)` | Attaches a design drawing (`diagnostics().drawings`); the mesh is unchanged. `model.render` draws its ortho-view curves and dimension spans on the drawing sheet |
| `drawingSvg(resolved, options?)`, `drawingDxf(resolved, options?)` | Sheet exports returning `Result<string, ModelingError>`: SVG in sheet millimeters (`scale` mm per m, default 1:10), one group per view and role, item ids as `data-id`, optional `measured` overlays; DXF R12 (AC1009, millimeters): a layer per role with ISO 128 line types, POLYLINE/VERTEX/SEQEND, POINT, TEXT, dimensions as LINE + TEXT. `views` and `columns` place the views. `sheet: { size: 'A0'..'A4', orientation?, title? }` adds an ISO 5457 sheet: border, zone marks, title block (caller text only; no clock) and a scale bar, and picks the largest ISO 5455 scale that fits when `scale` is omitted (`model-precondition` with `{needed, available}` mm when it cannot fit). `layers: true` gives every role group the id `layer:<view>:<role>` and `data-layer`; `projection: 'first' | 'third'` places the orthographic views as the projection method does (A1/A0 multi-view sheets); roles `visible` (solid) and `hidden` (dashed) carry `meshViewLines` output; a note with `balloon` and `deviation` (meters) shows the signed value under its text, red past the note's `tolerance`, and keys no table row. `grid` draws labelled lines at multiples of a spacing in meters. Section curves are hatched at 45 degrees with `hatchSpacing(segments)` (extent/40, shared with the capture Worker). A `{ id, of, detail: { box, scale, label? } }` view is an enlarged window of its parent, with a dashed mark in the parent. A note with `at` is placed on the sheet with a leader to its first ref; other notes are drawn under their view with a leader from their first ref. Point labels take free corners (a leader when crowded) and print without their `@view` suffix; angles print in degrees |
| `{ id, toward, up? }` view, `lookFrame(toward, up = +y)` | A 3/4 view: the viewer stands on the `toward` side of the subject, image up as close to `up` as the direction allows. `lookFrame` is the one right-handed frame (`right = up x toward`) that the perspective views also use; a zero, non-finite or `up`-parallel direction is `model-degenerate`, a malformed vector `model-invalid-argument`. `silhouetteIoU` and the `validate({ drawing })` silhouette check accept it; sheets title it `toward x,y,z` |
| role `material` note | A callout naming a slot of the mesh (`text`). `validate({ drawing })` casts a ray from the first ref point (which needs a `depth`) along `-toward`, and the first face's material name must equal the text, else a `material` finding with `expected` and `found`. A point without `depth`, or one beside the subject, yields a finding with `expected` and no `found`: unmeasured, never a pass |
| note `balloon: true` | A circle of fixed radius (3.5 sheet mm) at `at` holding `text`, with a leader from `refs[0]`; `text` must key column 0 of a table row of the same view, else `model-invalid-argument` at resolve. SVG `<circle data-id>`, DXF `CIRCLE` |
| item `kind: 'table'`, `drawingTable(id, view, source)` | `{ columns, rows }` of cells, placed under the view's notes with the same `line` height; column widths follow the longest cell at 0.6 x text height. `drawingTable` derives rows from `{ kind: 'dts', rows, report? }` (zone defaults merged with row limits, plus a `status` column with a gauge report), `{ kind: 'joints', joints }` (kind, axis and limits per joint, in the order given) or `{ kind: 'bom', parts }` (exploded parts). More than `TABLE_CELL_LIMIT` (4000) cells is `model-work-budget`, a ragged row or an empty source `model-invalid-argument` |
| `drawingCurve3(resolved, link, { tolerance?, controlPoints? })` | The space curve of a link drawn in two ortho views that share one world axis (side + plan share z, front + side y, front + plan x): each view's curve is read along the shared axis, the third coordinates meet at common stations and a cubic NURBS is fitted (`nurbs.approximate`, the fewest control points from 4 to 32 within the items' tightest tolerance). Register it with `m.featureLine({ name: link, curve })`. A view not single-valued along the shared axis, or no overlap, is `model-precondition`; no such view pair is `model-invalid-argument` |
| `validate({ drawing })` | The state against every attached drawing; tolerances are the items' own (default 2 mm). `section` curves in a section view: the state is cut at the station (`planeCut` on a copy, `sectionSegments`) and compared both ways, the cut counting against a drawn piece only inside its span (a band left out between pieces, such as a roll, counts against none); `silhouette` closed curves in an ortho view: `silhouetteIoU` below 0.93; `feature` curves whose `link` names a session feature line: the line projected into the view, both ways over the span both share (3D lines are often split or inset at panel shuts); `dimension` items: the span their refs draw against `value`. Each failure is a `drawing` warning, `detail` a `DrawingFinding` (`check`, `drawing`, `item`, `view`, `tolerance`, measures); a feature link with no line or a station the state does not cross reports no measures. `{ reach }` (default 0.05 m) bounds how far a cut or projected point may lie from a drawn piece and still count |
| `silhouetteIoU(model \| fxem, view, reference, cell?)`, `maskIoU(a, b)` | Silhouette overlap of a session (or FXEM snapshot) with a reference: an outline drawn in an ortho view, or another session or snapshot (U86, such as the high a low is checked against). Ortho views rasterise on one grid over the joint bounds (`cell`, default 5 mm). A `PerspectiveView` `{ eye, target, up? (+Y), fovY? (50 degrees), pixels? (256) }` rasterises both meshes into its own square image (`cell` unused); it needs a mesh reference and every vertex in front of the eye (`model-precondition`). Result: `{ iou, onlyA, onlyB }` (A = the reference, B = the subject). `maskIoU` is the one IoU core on equal-size boolean masks; image metrics use it too |
| `captureModels(body, { steps: true })` | Keeps an FXEM snapshot after every successful operation (`diagnostics().steps`, at most 64 MiB, then `stepsTruncated`): the source of step replay |
| `sectionSegments`, `evenOddFill`, `evenOddHatch` | Section outline of a plane cut (boundary edges on the plane) and its even-odd cap and hatch: nested outlines stay holes, which an n-gon cap cannot express; where separate shells overlap, the overlap also reads as a hole |
| `explodeMesh(mesh, { by, distance, directions?, order? })` | Copy with parts (faces grouped by `tag`, `label`, `material` or connected `island`) moved apart: authored directions, else away from the area-weighted center (`order: string[][]` puts step k at k x `distance`; unlisted parts stay at the base, a part in two steps is `model-invalid-argument`, an unknown part `model-selection-empty`; each `ExplodedPart` reports its `order`) |
| view `{ id, of, explode: { by, distance, directions?, order? } }`, `explodeDrawing(mesh, drawing)` | An exploded view of an ortho, section or toward view (not a detail view). `explodeDrawing` appends derived items to every explode view: per part `<view>/<part>/outline-<k>` silhouettes (white-filled, painted back to front, no hidden-line removal, said in the cell title) and `crease-<k>` features, a balloon per part and a `<view>/bom` table (`item, part, order, faces, offset`); rerunning it on its own output is `model-invalid-argument` (duplicate id). The 3D `explodeMesh` offsets and the sheet use the same options, so they agree to the byte |
| `meshViewEdges(mesh, frame)` | Front-facing contour, sharp and boundary edges of a mesh in a view frame, chained into polylines; it drops back faces only and has no hidden-line removal, use `meshViewLines` (`chain` is shared with drawing checks) |
| `meshViewLines(mesh, view, { id, creaseAngle?, sharp?, workLimit? })` | True hidden-line removal in an orthographic view (name or frame): contour, crease (angle >= `creaseAngle`, default 30 degrees, or sharp-flagged) and boundary edges are clipped exactly against every other triangle through a uniform grid and returned as `visible` / `hidden` `DrawnLine`s (ids `<id>/<kind>-<role>-<k>`, `basis: 'derived'`), hidden pieces on a visible line dropped. `Result`: `model-work-budget` past `workLimit` (default `HLR_WORK_LIMIT` grid cells plus edge-triangle tests) or the drawing point limit, `model-invalid-argument` for bad options |
